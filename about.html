Why we exist

Built to be different from day one

For too long, patients requiring biologic and specialty infusion therapy have had to navigate an experience that was designed around hospital systems — not around them. Long waits in busy infusion suites. Rotating nurses who don't know their history. Billing surprises after the fact. Care that was clinically sound but felt impersonal.

"We believed the Hudson Valley deserved better. A center where clinical rigor and genuine compassion aren't a trade-off — they're the standard."

HV Infusion & Biologics was built from the ground up with a different philosophy. Every decision — from our suite design to our nursing model to our billing process — was made with the patient's experience as the primary consideration. Not throughput. Not volume. The patient.

We serve Fishkill, Dutchess County, and the greater Hudson Valley — a community that has historically had to travel far for the quality of specialty infusion care its patients deserve. That changes here.

Our approach

The principles we practice every day

These aren't values on a wall. They're the specific commitments that shape every decision we make — from how we design our suites to how we handle a billing question.

01
Clinical excellence is non-negotiable
Biologic infusions are complex, high-stakes therapies. Our nursing team is trained specifically in infusion therapy and biologic medications — not generalists covering a broad floor. We monitor every infusion closely, manage reactions swiftly, and coordinate with your physician at every step. Clinical rigor is the foundation everything else is built on.
02
The patient experience is a clinical outcome
Patients who feel comfortable, respected, and informed are more likely to adhere to their treatment plans. That's not just good hospitality — it's good medicine. We invest in our environment, our communication, and our care culture because we believe how a patient feels during treatment directly affects how well they do.
03
We know our patients by name — not chart number
Long-term infusion therapy means recurring visits — sometimes for years. We build genuine relationships with our patients. We know their preferences, their histories, their families. That continuity isn't a luxury. It's how you catch a subtle change, earn trust, and deliver care that actually feels like care.
04
Transparency in everything — especially billing
Billing surprises are one of the most stressful parts of specialty care. We eliminate them. Before your first visit, you will know what your insurance covers, what prior authorization status is, and what your out-of-pocket cost will be. No assumptions. No guesswork. No surprises after the fact.
05
Physicians are our partners, not just referral sources
Your referring physician made a clinical decision to put their patient in our hands. We take that trust seriously. We communicate proactively, send detailed reports after every infusion, and keep your office informed of anything that changes. We are an extension of your practice — not a black box your patients disappear into.
06
Accessibility for the Hudson Valley community
Specialty infusion care shouldn't require a two-hour drive to a major medical center. We are here — in Fishkill — to serve Dutchess County and the surrounding region with a level of clinical excellence that patients previously had to leave for. Same-week appointments, extended hours, and proactive insurance support are all part of making this care accessible.
