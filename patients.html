Your first visit

What to expect at
HV Infusion & Biologics

We've designed the entire experience to feel effortless — from your first call to your last infusion drop. Here's what the process looks like.

1
Your physician refers you
Your doctor sends us a referral by phone, fax, or our online form. We receive it and begin the process immediately — no waiting around for paperwork to move.
2
We handle insurance for you
Our team verifies your benefits, obtains prior authorization, and contacts you with a clear explanation of what's covered before you ever set foot in our center.
3
We schedule your appointment
We contact you directly to find a time that works — often the same week. We're open Monday through Saturday with flexible morning and afternoon slots.
4
Arrive & check in
Check in at the front desk, settle into your private or semi-private suite, and meet your dedicated infusion nurse. No crowded waiting rooms — we're ready when you arrive.
5
Your infusion
Your nurse will review your medication, answer any questions, and start your infusion. We monitor you closely throughout and are with you every step of the way.
6
Follow-up & next visit
After your infusion we send a full report to your physician and help schedule your next appointment. We make recurring treatment as seamless as possible.
What to bring
For your first visit, please bring the following:
✓ Photo ID (driver's license or passport)
✓ Insurance card(s)
✓ List of current medications
✓ Referral paperwork (if given by your doctor)
✓ Any co-pay or cost-share amount confirmed with us in advance
What to bring for comfort
Infusions can take 1–4+ hours. Feel free to bring:
✓ A book, tablet, or laptop
✓ Headphones
✓ A light snack or meal
✓ A blanket or sweater (we provide these too)
✓ A companion — guests are welcome
Pro tip: Eat a good meal and drink plenty of water before your infusion. Well-hydrated veins make the IV process smoother and faster.

How the billing process works

No surprises — we
walk you through it all

Before your first infusion, we make sure you fully understand your coverage and any out-of-pocket costs.

1
Benefits verification
We contact your insurance company to confirm your infusion benefits and determine your deductible, co-pay, and co-insurance amounts.
2
Prior authorization

Frequently asked questions

Questions patients ask us most

How long does an infusion appointment take? +
Infusion times vary depending on the medication and your individual treatment plan. Most biologic infusions take between 1 and 4 hours. Your physician and our nursing team will give you a specific estimate based on your medication. We recommend planning for a little extra time on your first visit as we complete intake paperwork and get you settled.
Do I need a referral from my doctor? +
Yes — a physician referral is required for all infusion treatments. Your doctor will send us a prescription and referral, which our team uses to verify your insurance and order your medication. If you're not sure how to ask your doctor, we're happy to help guide you. Call us at (845) 848-3626.
Will my insurance cover infusion at your center? +
We accept most major commercial insurance plans and Medicare. Our billing team will verify your benefits before your first appointment and provide a clear cost estimate. In many cases, receiving infusion therapy at an outpatient center like ours is significantly less expensive than a hospital setting — which can benefit both you and your insurer.
Can I bring a family member or friend? +
Absolutely. Guests are welcome and we encourage it — having a companion can make long infusion sessions much more comfortable. We have seating available for guests in each suite. Please let us know when scheduling if you're planning to bring someone.
What should I eat before my infusion? +
We recommend eating a full, balanced meal before your infusion and drinking plenty of water. Good hydration helps with IV access and can reduce the likelihood of side effects. Avoid heavy, fatty foods right before your infusion. If you have specific dietary requirements related to your condition or medication, your physician will advise you accordingly.
What happens if I have a reaction during my infusion? +
Our nursing staff is trained in infusion reaction management and emergency response. We monitor you closely throughout your infusion and are prepared to manage any reaction promptly. In the rare event of a serious reaction, we have emergency protocols in place and will contact your physician immediately. Your safety is our first priority.
How do I reschedule or cancel an appointment? +
Please give us at least 24 hours notice if you need to reschedule or cancel. Because your medication is ordered specifically for your appointment, last-minute cancellations can result in wasted medication. Call us at (845) 848-3626 as soon as possible and we'll find a new time that works for you.
