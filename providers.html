Why refer to HV Infusion

What sets us apart as
your infusion partner

We've built our center around making physician partnerships seamless — so your patients receive expert care and you stay informed without extra administrative burden.

01
Biologic specialization
We administer biologics and specialty infusions exclusively — your patients are treated by nurses who know these medications deeply, not generalists covering everything.
02
Rapid patient access
We schedule most new patients within the same week of receiving a referral. We handle insurance verification and prior auth so nothing slows treatment down.
03
Full transparency
You receive detailed treatment reports after every infusion. We communicate directly with your office on any changes, reactions, or concerns — no information silos.
04
Cost-effective setting
Ambulatory infusion centers are significantly less expensive than hospital outpatient settings — better for your patients' wallets and their insurer's coverage limits.
The referral process

Simple from your end.
Thorough on ours.

We've made the referral process as frictionless as possible. Submit online, by phone, or by fax — we take it from there.

Submit a referral now
1
You submit a referral
Use our online referral form (takes under 5 minutes), call us at (845) 848-3626, or fax referral documents. We accept referrals for all patients regardless of prior infusion history.
2
We confirm receipt & begin intake
Your office receives a confirmation within one business day. Our team begins benefits verification and prior authorization immediately — we reach out if we need any additional clinical documentation.
3
Insurance & medication coordination
We obtain prior authorization from the insurer, coordinate medication delivery from specialty pharmacy, and confirm the patient's cost-share before scheduling. You don't need to manage any of this.
4
Patient is scheduled & seen
We contact the patient directly to schedule their first appointment — often same week. Your office is notified of the scheduled date and time.
5
Treatment report sent to your office
After each infusion, we send a detailed report to your office including medications administered, infusion duration, any reactions or observations, and next scheduled appointment. Your patient's chart stays complete.

Communication & reporting

You stay in the loop — always

We believe in tight-loop communication with referring physicians. Your patients are your patients — we just administer the therapy.

Post-infusion treatment reports
After every infusion we send a detailed report to your office — medication administered, infusion time, patient vitals, any observations or reactions, and next scheduled date.
Direct clinical communication
Our nurses and clinical coordinator are available directly by phone. If your patient has a reaction, a question, or needs a dose adjustment, we contact you immediately — not through a portal three days later.
Prior auth status updates
We keep your office informed on prior authorization status — no more chasing insurance companies. We notify you when auth is approved and when your patient is scheduled.
